Your Reconnection Blueprint: Words of Affirmation

How to get them to come back, and help them get over their grudges 2 to 3 times faster.

What Their Love Language Means

If their main love language is Words of Affirmation, it means the most important thing to them is sincere, genuine compliments and spoken or written encouragement.

Imagine if someone’s love language was physical touch and they were slapped or physically abused… That would be extremely harsh on that individual.

If someone’s love language is Words of Affirmation, and they are called names, dismissed, or have their ideas downplayed—or if someone tears down their character—from their perspective, it is just as harsh.

How to Help Them Forgive & Let Go of Grudges 2x–3x Faster

Here is exactly what you need to do if you want to restore the relationship and get them to forgive you and get over their grudges 2 to 3 times faster:

A minimum of 15 minutes every single day (that is not an exaggeration), you need to plan and implement their love language.

You can write little notes that they will find around the house, give them sincere and genuine compliments throughout the day, or write them one thoughtful, heartfelt letter.

Obviously you are not going to lie about these... think about things that they do well, if they are trying hard at work, if they do care deeply about something, if they are good at something they like a lot... those are the compliments that you give them.

These things must be done consistently. And if there is an argument or a fight that day, you double down on their love language—literally—and implement it for at least 30 minutes that day.

The 4 Stages Your Loved One Will Go Through

When you commit to doing this every single day, your partner or loved one will walk through four predictable stages:

1 Suspicion & Disbelief

At first, they will think you are suspect. They will not believe you, and they will assume you are up to something.

→ KEEP DOING IT! Every single day!
2 Suspicion But Curious

They will still think you are suspect, but they will start to dip their toe in the water. They will be highly skeptical and wonder why you are doing what you’re doing—but everyone loves their love language, and everyone wants to be treated that way, so they will be curious enough to see why you are doing it.

3 Angry and Frustrated

They will feel angry that you didn’t do this sooner. They will think to themselves, “If they knew I liked to be treated this way, why weren’t they doing this all along?!”

→ KEEP DOING IT! Every single day!
4 Introspective

They will realize that you don’t have a hidden agenda—that you genuinely love them. At that point, they will recognize they have been holding onto a grudge, and they will actively seek to let it go.

These four stages are helpful and you WILL go through these. At any time, if you want to speed up how fast you are going through these four stages with your partner, DOUBLE DOWN on their love language! A minimum of 30 minutes to implement their love language every day.

The Problem Most People Have With Implementing Their Partners Love Language:

The biggest problem most people have with implementing their partner’s love language is fear: they don’t want to give their partner the impression that what they did was okay. They don’t want their partner to think they can get away with hurtful behavior, that all is automatically forgiven, or that they don’t need to make any real changes.

People also hesitate because they wonder: “What about me? When do I get to feel my love language? Why do I always have to be the one giving first, and when do I get mine filled?”

That is NOT what the love language is for.

Speaking their love language is the bridge that helps them trust you again, know you have their best interest at heart, and feel safe enough to be vulnerable and want to collaborate with you.

How to Actually Fix the Root Problems & Unlock Your Relationship Superpower

If you want to actually right the wrongs, see real change, stop repeating the same exact cycles, make sure you are never treated like a doormat—you must figure out what your conation is, and what their conation is.

When you understand their natural conation and yours, you immediately see how the two of you are wired to work together—you unlock your superpower. When you don’t, you see your points of friction, where you clash, and your blind spots.

This will work with absolutely ANYONE in 60 days or less- you will start to notice in the first couple of days! I promise. You will have some hard conversations with them. But pairing conation with their love language is guaranteed to heal any relationship, show your superpowers when you work together, and how to avoid friction points and blind spots.
